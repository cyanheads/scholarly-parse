# 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

## Added

- **Format parsers, one subpath each** — `parseJats` (`scholarly-parse/jats`), `parseTei` (`/tei`, Grobid TEI including OpenAlex's HTML-wrapped form), `parseLatexml` (`/latexml`, arXiv HTML and ar5iv), `parseHtml` (`/html`, publisher article pages), and `parsePdf` (`/pdf`, PDFs with a text layer). The XML parsers are synchronous; HTML and PDF are async and need the optional peers `linkedom` and `unpdf`.
- **`detect`** names a payload's format from its opening bytes; **`parse`** runs that format's parser, loaded on first use.
- **`ScholarlyDocument`** — the model every parser produces: front matter (including related works, such as the article a correction corrects or the notice retracting it), abstracts by kind, a section tree of typed blocks (paragraph, list, table, figure, formula, code, quote, box, supplement), references, footnotes, floats, and `diagnostics` with a `quality` of `structured`, `partial`, or `flat`, coded warnings, and unhandled source elements.
- **`ParseResult`** — parsers return `{ ok: true, document }` or `{ ok: false, error: { reason, message } }`, with reasons `malformed`, `wrong-format`, `empty`, `blocked` (a captcha or access-denied page served in the paper's place), and `too-large` (over `maxInputBytes`).
- **`toMarkdown`**, **`toText`**, **`toSections`** — Markdown with `MarkdownOptions` to choose abstracts, back matter, footnotes, references, or specific sections; plain text; and a flat section list with paths and sizes for paging a long paper.
- **`jatsInlineToMarkdown`** converts a JATS fragment, such as a Crossref or Europe PMC abstract, without an article around it.
- **Corpus** — 71 openly licensed documents (38 JATS, 11 LaTeXML, 8 TEI, 8 PDF, 6 publisher HTML) run through their parsers on every test, each held to the corpus invariants, assertions of its own, and a reviewed Markdown snapshot.
